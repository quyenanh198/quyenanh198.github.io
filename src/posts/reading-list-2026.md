A few things I read recently that were worth the time:

- **On writing clearly** — the more technical the subject, the more the plain sentence wins over the clever one.
- **On small tools** — software doesn't need an audience to be worth building. Some of the most useful things I've written were never meant to be shared.
- **On design systems** — consistency is a discipline you re-apply constantly, not a document you write once.

> Simplicity is not the absence of clutter... simplicity is about the right thing, in the right place, for the right reason.

I'll keep adding to this list as the year goes on.
